These are proposed starting values and have not been confirmed yet.

  • Each order counts once per action, for the staff member who did it first. Re-sending, double clicks or switching a status back and forth never add points.
  • “Moved from Processing” means an order left the Processing tab (Pending, Processing, Shipping, Courier) to Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, Returned, Refunded or Trash. Sending a parcel from Processing also moves the order to Confirmed.
  • “Incomplete converted” is the Incomplete Orders page’s Send / Add Order action.
  • Changing the points recalculates every period. Actions are recorded from the day this feature went live.
